Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Command Center is a desktop app that runs on the user's own Windows PC.

Google data

  • When you connect a Google account, the app asks for access to your calendar events and your list of calendars, so it can show them and add, change or delete events you choose.
  • Calendar data is fetched directly from Google to your PC and shown in the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, not sold, and not used for advertising.
  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only in the app's local database on your PC.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnect the account in the app (Settings › Google accounts), or remove "Command Center" at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes its local data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/papioshu/command-center.
